# Version 31 - Advanced Offline Spreadsheet

## Landscape focus mode

When Focus mode is active and the phone is landscape:
- the sheet title and description are hidden;
- the bottom status text is hidden;
- toolbar labels collapse to icons;
- the Exit control stays in the toolbar;
- the grid uses the freed vertical space.

## Multiple-cell selection

Phone/tablet:
1. Tap the first cell.
2. Tap **Range**.
3. Tap the opposite corner.

Desktop:
- Shift+click extends a selection.
- Shift+Arrow extends the selected range.
- Escape returns to one-cell selection.

Selected ranges can be copied as tab-separated values, cleared, formatted as RM, or merged.

## Pinch zoom

Use two fingers over the grid to zoom between 65% and 160%. The sheet tries to keep the pinch center in view. The zoom level is stored locally in the browser.
